# Design System: Influstep

## 1. Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Quiet Control Room"**

Influstep is a product surface the operator opens several times a day, often on a phone, to answer three questions: who answered me, what is worth answering now, and is it working. The chrome stays out of the way; Today and the report carry the page. It inherits Formstep's tokens (paper, ink, one green) so the two products read as siblings, and adds a **status tint scale** for drafts, posts, replies and comments, because Today is a list of things in different states and their state must be legible at a glance.

**Grouping is the layout.** What belongs together sits on one card, paper over the canvas, so the operator never has to work out which line goes with which. A page is a few cards, never a wall of rules: alerts first (what failed, what waits on the operator), then the work in tabs or cards, then what is secondary in collapsibles.

Rejected: dashboard grids of a dozen equal widgets, colored KPI tiles, gradient funnels, "AI sparkle" iconography, chat bubbles styled like a consumer messenger, feed readers that copy a platform's look, cards inside cards. Reference points: Linear's inbox, Notion's calendar, Stripe's reports.

**Key characteristics**

- One Inter family. JetBrains Mono for counts, timestamps, handles, slugs, and job payloads.
- Tinted neutrals. `#000` and `#fff` never appear as tokens.
- One saturated color in the chrome: Field Green means "working" (an account listening, an item posted, an experiment decided). Red means failed or blocked, amber means waiting on the operator, blue means informational. Never more than one role per element.
- An account is its platform's logo and its handle, never an avatar or an identity color. The logo is monochrome graphite, so it never competes with a status.
- Hierarchy by weight and opacity, not by grey text colors (One Ink Rule).
- Flat by default: a card has a mist hairline and a 1px shadow at 4% ink, nothing more; deeper shadows only on popovers and dialogs.
- Density: product-compact. 32px controls, 14px body, 12-column grid, 16px gutters at phone width.

## 2. Colors

Same palette as Formstep (see frontmatter). Rules that matter here:

**Status tint scale.** Each status of a post, a reply or a comment has one tint, applied as a 6% background wash and a 100% dot on cards and as a pill in lists. Never a side stripe. Tints are the semantic colors, never new hues:

| Status            | Tint        | Reads as                          |
| ----------------- | ----------- | --------------------------------- |
| idea, heard       | fog         | not yet real                      |
| draft             | alert-amber | waiting on you: post it or skip it |
| posted            | field-green | out, being measured               |
| measured          | ink         | done, in the report               |
| skipped, killed   | fog         | passed on, struck through         |
| failed            | signal-red  | look at this                      |
| comment new       | alert-amber | someone answered you              |
| comment seen      | note-blue   | read, not answered yet            |
| comment answered  | field-green | you answered                      |
| comment dismissed | fog         | let go                            |

**Surfaces.** Three layers: the canvas (`canvas`, one step under paper) behind every page, paper for cards, paper-tint for the sidebar and for a card's action band. Status tints never fill a surface; they wash a post card or an alert.

**One saturated voice per screen.** Today shows every tint at once because they are content. The chrome around it (nav, buttons, headers) stays ink on paper.

**Character counter.** A post part counts toward X's 280 as the operator types, the way X counts (every link weighs 23). Under 90 percent of the limit the count is ash; from 90 percent up to the limit itself it turns alert-amber-deep, a warning the operator acts on; past the limit it turns signal-red-deep, because X refuses the part and the studio will not save it. The count is 11px mono (0.6875rem), smaller than the Mono role so it sits beside the 12px part label; the deep shades keep those small numbers legible on paper. Amber and red keep their meaning here (look at this, blocked); they never wash a background or mark a status. Color is not the only signal: the field names its counter as its description, and the counter is a polite live region that says "near the limit" or "over the limit" in words. A reply draft counts by the same rule.

**Charts.** The report's funnel and trend lines use the chart series in canonical order: cyan, violet, amber, emerald, red. Followers always cyan, views violet, clicks amber, signups emerald, paid red. Fixed assignment, so the operator learns the colors once. Demos and trials are the lighter shade (55%) of the step they lead from: demos of clicks amber, trials of signups emerald.

## 3. Typography

- **Page title** Inter 600, 1.5rem, tracking -0.02em. One per page.
- **Card title** Inter 600, 0.9375rem (`text-title`), with its count as a mono chip beside it.
- **Meta** Inter 400, 0.75rem, ash: one line under a title, the hint under a field, the line under a list.
- **Body** Inter 400, 0.875rem, line-height 1.5.
- **Metric eyebrow** Inter 600, 0.6875rem, uppercase, tracking 0.18em. Reserved for the report's metric strip, the sidebar's Accounts label and the setup's welcome; a section is a card with a card title, not an eyebrow.
- **Metric value** Inter 600, 1.5rem, tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- **Mono** JetBrains Mono 0.8125rem: handles (`@automation.ada`), slugs, timestamps, counts in tables, job payloads.

## 5. Components

- **Button primary**: ink on paper-tint, 2rem high, rounded lg. Hover ink-deep.
- **Busy button**: any button whose request is out shows a spinner beside its label, says so to assistive tech (aria-busy) and ignores clicks, a form's submit included; it is never disabled for being busy, since that drops its focus.
- **Field**: the label above its control, the hint under it in 12px ash, outside the label, so the hint describes the control instead of being read as part of its name.
- **Attribution note** (reports, post page): one 12px ash line in view (platform counts, exact clicks, signups and paid from PostHog first touch, which undercounts), a disclosure that opens the full paragraph, so the caveat is never out of sight and never pushes the funnel down.
- **Button ghost**: transparent, hover hairline-strong.
- **Button destructive**: signal-red-deep text with a 10% signal-red wash on hover, used only for Kill, Pause, an experiment's Stop, and Clear history with its Delete history confirm.
- **Pill**: status tint at 18% background, deep tint foreground, rounded pill, 1.25rem high.
- **Card**: paper over the canvas, a mist hairline, rounded xl, a 1px shadow at 4% ink; 16px padding on a phone, 20px from 640px. Its header is a card title with an optional count chip, one 12px ash line under it, and its actions at the right; a flush card hands its body the full width for a list whose rows pad themselves between mist hairlines; a footer is a mist-ruled band of 12px ash. A card never sits inside another card. A card that holds a form or a draft ends with an action band (`card-band`): paper-tint under a mist rule, the actions in one row. A table in a flush card (`card-table`) gives its first and last cells the card's own side padding.
- **Alert**: what the operator must see before the rest of the page. A full 1px border at 35% of the tone's tint and a 7% wash mixed in sRGB (so blue never drifts green through the hue wheel), the tone's filled Phosphor icon (Info, Warning, WarningCircle, CheckCircle) in its deep shade, a title at weight 500 and one line in graphite, and at most one action at the right (under the words on a phone). Tones: danger (signal-red) for a failure, warning (alert-amber) for what waits on the operator, info (note-blue), success (field-green). Never a side stripe.
- **Tabs**: views of one thing (Today's lists, an account's pages). Links, not a tablist, so each view has its address: 40px high, 14px at weight 500, the current one in ink over a 2px ink rule, the rest ash; a count chip beside each label; the row scrolls sideways on a phone. Panels of an in-page tab stay mounted and are hidden, so a half-edited draft survives a switch. A page that already sits under tabs (an account's page) splits its own views with segmented tabs instead: a hairline-strong track, 28px items at 13px weight 500, the current one raised on paper with a 1px shadow.
- **Meter**: how far a count has come toward its whole, a 4px fully rounded mist track filled in graphite; hidden from screen readers, since the words beside it say the same.
- **Collapsible**: what the operator opens only when needed. A `details` with a caret that turns 90° over 150ms, the summary at 14px weight 500 in graphite with its count chip and an optional 12px ash line at the right; nothing interactive sits in the summary.
- **Count chip**: a number beside a title or a tab, 11px mono graphite on hairline-strong, fully rounded. Never a status; a count that waits on the operator is the sidebar's amber count.
- **Post card** (Today): one post. The status tint as a 6% wash with a dot before the first line, and a 35% tint border when the card needs the operator: a draft (shared `needsOperator`) or a failed post; hovering any card draws its border at 40%. Then the kind icon and the post's first line (its hook) in 13px at weight 500, clamped to two lines; before the writer has written the post, the planned title stands in. Under it, indented past the icon, the post's intent and length in 11px ash ("Ask · One-liner"), an idea's idea line, and the sponsor segment ("Sponsor mention") when the post names the sponsor; then a failed card's error (one line, mono, signal-red-deep), on a draft the judge found too few reasons in "No reason to comment or follow" (one 11px alert-amber-deep line), and one meta row: the account's handle in mono, truncated, and the time the post is due at the right. A killed card fades to 60% and strikes its line through.
- **Idea line**: an idea has no words yet, so where a written post shows its first line it says when the writer gets to them: "Writing on Wed, Oct 14", the day before it goes out (shared `writeDayOf`), or "Writing now" while its write job is queued or running. On the post card it is an 11px ash line under the first line, indented past the kind icon; in an account's post list it takes the hook's line. The post page says it once, in a 12px ash line under the header with the rule in the account's words: "I write the post the day before it goes out, after a last look at what moved that morning."
- **Intent and length**: what the planner asked of a post, as plain words joined by a middle dot: the intent (Ask, Inform, Share, Teach), then the length (One-liner, Post, Thread). Small and ash wherever it appears, on the post card and after the kind in the post page's header; never a pill, never a control, because the operator edits the post, not its plan.
- **Kind icons**: Phosphor, regular weight, 14px, graphite. TextT for a text post, ListNumbers for a thread. Used on the post card, in an account's post list and in the post page's header. The icon is hidden from screen readers and the kind's name is its text alternative: printed beside the icon in the post page's header, screen-reader only elsewhere, so the kind is never read twice.
- **Post editor** (post page): the draft as the operator will post it on X. A Title field first, hinted "For the calendar and the report; X shows only the post.", then a text post's one textarea, labelled Post, or a thread's two to six numbered parts with Add part and a ghost Remove on each part's label row (Remove off at two parts, Add part off at six). Every part's label row carries a character counter, `len / 280` in 11px mono, colored by the character counter rule above. Adding a part moves focus to its textarea; removing one moves focus to the part before it (or the new first part), so keyboard focus never falls back to the top of the page. Before the writer has run, the editor opens with the fewest empty parts the kind allows, so the operator can write the post by hand. Under the parts the post's problems as the operator types (a polite live list), then Save post (primary while there is an unsaved edit, outline otherwise, off while there is nothing to save or a problem) beside the 12px ash line "X counts every link as 23 characters." The text stays editable while the post is an idea, a draft or failed (shared `textEditable`); after that the fields lock and the line adds why: "Locked once you posted it.", or for a killed or skipped post "This post was let go, so it is locked." A refused save shows as an error line under the form. On a draft the editor ends, outside its form so Enter in the link never saves instead, with **Waiting on you** on a faint alert-amber wash, rounded xl: its eyebrow in alert-amber-deep, one line ("Post it on X yourself, as it stands above, then say so here. Influstep never posts."), then "Link to the post" (optional; the hint says the analyst reads the post's numbers with it) with the link's problem in 12px signal-red-deep under it as it is typed, then I posted it (primary, busy while it saves, off while the post has a problem or the link names no X post), and a refusal as an error line. I posted it records the title and parts as they stand in the editor, saved or not.
- **Format fields** (onboarding, account settings): one fieldset for the account's one platform, its legend the platform's kind label, between mist rules: First post at (the local hour), Posts a day (its hint lists the hours the posts fall on), Post kinds (Text post, Thread) as checkboxes, Replies a day (hinted "Drafts for you to post", or "Off: listens to nobody" at 0) and Post intents (Ask, Inform, Share, Teach) as checkboxes; the last ticked kind or intent cannot be cleared, since the backend refuses a format without one.
- **Input / Textarea**: hairline background, rounded lg, 2rem high (textarea auto-grows).
- **Chat**: operator messages right-aligned on paper-warm, account replies left on paper with a hairline and the account mark beside them; the directives in force sit pinned above the composer under their own eyebrow, each with Withdraw. The composer's placeholder names what the chat can do: "Message <name>: ask, give a rule, or change the plan". The account reads every message and answers with what it did. A reply that changed a post (replaced, moved or dropped) ends inside its bubble with the post's title as a link at weight 500, underlined in fog (ink on hover). A reply that handed a task to an agent shows, under its bubble while the job waits or runs, one 12px ash line with a quiet spinner: "Queued for the researcher", then "The researcher is on it". The line goes once the job is done or failed, because the answer, or why it could not be given, arrives as an account message of its own. It sits in a polite live region under the bubble that stays mounted while the line comes and goes, so each step is announced; the message list itself is no live region, so nothing is read twice. The "directive" mark stays on the operator's message only. The hint under the composer says "Reads like a directive. Once taken as one, it stays in force until you withdraw it." when the shared plain-English parser reads the text as one, else "Cmd+Enter to send"; the account decides once it reads it, so the hint never promises.
- **Checklist item**: a checkbox (ticking it marks the item done), the title, the why, then an 11px mono ash line with the account's handle linking its page and when it was made; at the right an optional action: Set <key> (outline, small) to the Providers page; Connect (outline, small) for an account its platform reads only once the operator lets it; or, for a page the operator must read and act on by hand, an outline "Open" link that opens in a new tab. On the account page the same item is shorter: the why clamped to two lines.
- **Account mark**: the platform's logo (Phosphor RedditLogo, XLogo or LinkedinLogo, bold, at 55% of the chip; Hacker News, which Phosphor lacks, is its Y set bold at the same size; every forum shares ChatsCircle, since the forum's name says which) in graphite on a round paper-warm chip with a mist hairline, always beside the account's name or handle (written as its platform writes it: @ada on X and on a forum, u/ada on Reddit, in/ada on LinkedIn, bare on Hacker News), which say whose it is, so the mark is hidden from screen readers. Sizes: 20px in chat, the Agents page's job rows and the report tables, 28px in the sidebar, 32px in lists, 48px in the account header. An account whose agents are working (a job queued or running) carries an 8px note-blue dot on the chip's bottom-right corner, ringed in paper. A paused account's logo is fog, and its chip carries no dot even with jobs waiting in its queue.
- **Length mix bar** (voice page): how a feed splits into one-liners, posts and threads. One stacked bar, 8px high, fully rounded, segments in POST_LENGTHS order with a 2px paper gap between them, each as wide as its share (a zero share draws no segment). Neutral tones by length, lightest to darkest: smoke for a one-liner, ash for a post, ink for a thread, so more words read as more ink. Never a chart series color, which the report owns, and never the account's color, which is identity. Under the bar a legend carries the numbers: an 8px swatch, the length's name and its whole percent in 11px mono ash. The bar is hidden from screen readers; the legend says the same in words. Without a learned voice the page draws the default mix the planner uses, and says so.
- **Import progress line** (voice page): one 12px line under an import tile that says where the import stands: "Reading the posts…", then "Sent 1,000 of 3,214 posts…" with the counts in 11px mono, then "Added 3,100 posts; 114 were already here." An archive is read in the browser and sent in batches one after another, so the count only goes up and a failure stops at a known point: the error shows as an error line, and when posts already went in, a line says that importing again skips them and sends the rest. It is a polite live region that stays mounted, empty while idle, so each step is announced without moving focus.
- **Inline confirm** (Clear history): a destructive action that cannot be undone asks under its own button, not in a modal: one sentence that says what is deleted and what is not ("The posts stay on X"), then Cancel (ghost) and the destructive button. Focus moves to Cancel when the question opens, and Cancel hands it back to the button. The confirmed action removes the line the button sat in, so focus moves to a status line that stays mounted (tabindex -1, a polite live region) and says what happened, never to the top of the page.
- **Example toggle** (voice page): a checkbox labeled "Example" at the right of an imported post, described by the post's text. Its state is the server's: it moves once the save lands, and a refused save says why on the row, in a polite live region that stays mounted. The examples and the top posts are one list with a "Top posts" separator (a presentation item holding the heading), so a ticked row moves within the list and focus stays on its box. While the save is out the box is aria-busy and ignores clicks; it is never disabled then, since that would drop focus. At eight examples an unmarked box is disabled, and the section's line says to untick one first.
- **Job state** (voice page, under Import from <platform> and Learn the voice; strategy page, under Review now): where the job a button queued stands, as one 11px mono ash line: a 6px dot, the status word and when it last moved ("Running 4 seconds ago"), with a failed job's error as an error line under it. Dot tints: field-green done, signal-red failed, note-blue running, fog queued; the account's and the post's job lists and the Agents page draw the same dot. Every status shows, done included. The line is not a live region itself: the caller wraps it in a polite live region that stays mounted, empty before the first job, so the job appearing and each change after it are announced.
- **Journal entry** (strategy page): one change to an account's strategy, so the operator can see why the account does what it does. A meta line in 11px ash (the date in mono, the kind of change, "by the analyst", "by the researcher" or "by you"), the change in one sentence at body size in ink, the reason under it in 12px ash, then a closed "Numbers and evidence" disclosure holding the numbers as label and value pairs in 11px (the label ash, the value mono graphite) and an "Evidence" line in 11px ash with the experiment, the posts and the signals it rests on as links underlined in fog (ink on hover); a signal links the Signals page filtered to the account, since a signal has no page of its own, and a post or a signal that no longer exists drops out of it. A swapped concept (the researcher's last check) reads like every other entry: the old and the new concept in the sentence, the freshness of each as its numbers, and the post and both signals as its evidence. No pill and no tint: a journal entry is a record, not a status, and every entry reads the same whoever made it.
- **Experiment line** (Experiments page): under each experiment's hypothesis, who chose it first: "Picked by <name>" when the account's strategy review started it, "Set up by you for <name>" when the operator did, then the handle in mono, the dimension and the metric ("Reply style on follows") and the decision rule ("decides at 500 exposures per variant, 90% confidence"). A running experiment has Decide myself (outline, small), which opens a Winner select, a Why field and Record decision (primary), and Stop (destructive, small); a refusal shows as an error line under the experiment. An experiment runs on an account's replies, so the new experiment form names the dimension (Reply style, Reply target) and offers the metrics a reply can be decided on, Views and Follows, Follows by default, since a reply on X carries no link. It fills its variants from the experiment catalog, what the account does now first as the control; the lines stay editable, and a new pick refills them only while they are empty or still the fill. A line that names a catalog variant keeps that variant's key, so the analyst's learnings and the account's defaults recognize it. A refusal shows as an error line under the form. Each experiment's variant table counts the replies that carry the variants and went out, under a column named Replies.
- **Product fields** (setup, Providers page): Product name and Website side by side on wide screens (an address typed without its scheme reads as https; the hint says tracked links send readers there), then "What it does, in one sentence" with its `len / 200` counter on the label row and an ash hint that the accounts say it in their own words, never as an ad read; then the value of one paying customer and an 8rem Currency field in mono. Checked by the shared `sponsorProblems`, the same words the backend refuses with. On the Providers page they sit under "Your product" with the Studio address (where tracked links live), all problems shown as the operator types and Save off while there are any.
- **Empty states**: one sentence and one action. No illustrations.

## 6. Motion

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: under it every animation and transition collapses to an instant. Buttons and post cards ease their colors over 150ms (ease-out-quint); the sidebar's width and the funnel's bars ease over 200ms; no spring on UI. Nothing animates while the operator reads a report.

Nothing loops.
